# Production Build Notes

## Research pipeline
1. Resolve company identity.
2. Retrieve reliable sources.
3. Rank sources by authority.
4. Extract candidate claims.
5. Validate every claim.
6. Store claim + URL + source type + dates + confidence.
7. Generate conversation angles only from validated claims.
8. Convert unavailable information into discovery gaps.

## Transcript pipeline
1. Parse transcript and speaker/timestamp metadata.
2. Extract explicit facts only.
3. Evaluate Need, Pain, Impact, Budget, Authority, Timeline, Current Solution, Decision Process and Next Step.
4. Compare against pre-call research and discovery gaps.
5. Identify meaningful missed opportunities.
6. Generate evidence-based coaching.
7. Generate next-call priorities and questions.

## Do not build into MVP
- Overall call quality score
- Buying probability
- CRM integration
- Automated outreach
- Unverified HRMS inference
- SDR leaderboard
